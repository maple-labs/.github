Too much capital sits dormant, held back by opacity, outdated rails, and gatekept access. Maple exists to activate it. We put capital to work where it creates growth, and we do it onchain, where anyone can see what we've lent and what backs it.

We're an onchain asset manager. Lenders allocate capital to Maple, our credit team underwrites every strategy we fund, and we deploy capital to where it performs, with every position verifiable onchain. The credit we provide funds genuine economic activity: borrowers build, businesses expand, and lenders earn yield.

We've underwritten through full market cycles since 2019, with over $25B originated across 400+ facilities, and more than $100M in yield distributed to lenders. **Maple Earn**, **Maple Credit**, and **Maple Embed** are three ways in, all powered by one onchain engine.
